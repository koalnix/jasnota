# Јаснота / Jasnota — brand foundation (v2)

Single source of truth for every Jasnota deliverable (identity, website, Instagram, brand kit).
**v2 (29 Oct 2026)** follows the founder's strategy in `STRATEGY.md`, which wins wherever anything differs.
v1 treated Jasnota as a personal artist brand; that framing is retired.

## What Jasnota is

**An informative, educational platform that explores and explains the connection between music, psychological
experience and human behaviour — through science-based content and an interdisciplinary approach.**

MK: Јаснота е информативна и едукативна платформа што ја истражува и објаснува поврзаноста меѓу музиката,
психолошкото искуство и човековото однесување преку научно засновани содржини и интердисциплинарен пристап.

- **Mission:** raise awareness of the psychology behind music; make scientific knowledge accessible,
  understandable and relevant for musicians and the wider public.
- **Vision:** a recognisable platform with an international perspective that connects psychology, musical practice,
  academic knowledge and professional communities.
- **Now (phase 1):** text-first. One quality piece a week, with sources. No events, no own research yet,
  **no personal video or public exposure needed** — the author is present through her voice and judgement.
- **Is not:** therapy, diagnosis or individual advice; a personal artist page; motivational or wellness content;
  a copy of existing accounts.

Descriptor (use under the logo, in bios, meta): **„Психологијата зад музиката“** / *The psychology behind music*.
Signature line (optional, quieter, for closings): „Да се слушнеш себеси.“ / *To hear yourself.*

## The name

- **јасна** — clear, lucid; also the founder's name. **нота** — a musical note.
- **Јасна нота** = a clear note → clarity about music. **Јаснота** echoes **јасност** (clarity) — the editorial promise:
  make complex research clear without making it wrong.
- It also splits as **јас + нота** — the self and the sound.
- Wordmark: **Cyrillic `јаснота` is primary** (Macedonian is the base language); Latin `jasnota` for English/international
  contexts and URLs/handles. Always lowercase in the wordmark; "Јаснота" / "Jasnota" in running text.

## Audience & languages

1. **Musicians** — students, performers, teachers, ensembles (Pillar 2 speaks to them directly).
2. **The wider public** curious about why music affects us (Pillar 1).
3. **Professionals** — psychologists, music educators, music therapists, researchers (Pillar 3 rigour, sources, mapping).

**Macedonian is the default language** of the website and social content. English versions exist for the interface
and for selected pieces; later, selected texts may be adapted into Serbian and Croatian. Each language must read as if
written in it.

## Principles (from the strategy) → what they mean on the page

- **Scientific grounding** → every factual claim cites a source (APA 7); evidence labels on claims:
  `силни докази` · `умерени докази` · `рани/ограничени докази` · `отворено прашање`.
  Each piece ends with **Што знаеме / Што сè уште не знаеме** and **Извори**.
- **Clarity** → plain Macedonian, the English term in brackets on first use; one idea per paragraph; no jargon without a definition.
- **Authenticity** → own voice, own questions; no reposted infographics, no copied formats.
- **Interdisciplinarity** → name the field a finding comes from (clinical, cognitive, music education, performance science).
- **Ethics** → never diagnosis, therapy or individual advice; a short "not therapy" note on psychology pieces with a referral
  line; personal stories only with consent and anonymised; no invented anecdotes.
- **International openness** → topics chosen so they travel; English versions of the strongest pieces.

## Voice

Clear, precise, warm, calm. **A careful scientist who knows the stage.** Explains, doesn't preach; shows uncertainty honestly
("истражувањата сугерираат", "доказите се мешани"); never sensational, never clickbait, no exclamation marks.
Editorial "we/ние" or impersonal on the website; direct "ти" in practical tips and on social. Short sentences.
The poetry lives in the design and in a single line, not in the facts.

Avoid: "unlock", "journey", "transform", "hack", "toxic", "trauma" used loosely, "studies show" without a source,
and any promise of cure or quick fix.

## Visual identity (unchanged from v1)

### Core ideas we design from
1. **Attunement / камертон (tuning fork)** — its silhouette is almost **Ψ** (psi): psychology and music in one sign.
2. **Asymmetric rhythm — 7/8** (2+2+3): Macedonian meters as the grid/spacing/timing motif.
3. **Silence / пауза** — negative space is a feature.
4. **Clarity / јасност** — one clear point of light on black.
5. **Roots** — Ohrid literary school geometry, fresco pigment; never folkloric kitsch.

### Palette — black first
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#0A0A0B` | Primary background |
| `--night` | `#131315` | Raised surfaces on black |
| `--graphite` | `#2A2A2D` | Hairlines, dividers on black |
| `--ash` | `#8C8A85` | Secondary text on black (not on light — use `#66645F` there) |
| `--bone` | `#ECE8E1` | Primary text on black; light background |
| `--paper` | `#F6F4EF` | Light surfaces (print, reading cards, source slides) |
| `--cinnabar` | `#B4452C` | Fresco-red accent, **< 2%**: one dot, one word, one line |

### Typography (Macedonian Cyrillic: Ѓ Ќ Ѕ Џ Љ Њ Ј)
- **Display:** Cormorant 300/400 + Italic (for one emotive word). Macedonian italics need the Serbian locl forms
  (`:lang(mk) :is(em,i) { font-language-override: "SRB" }`).
- **Text:** Onest 300–400. Long reads: comfortable measure (60–70ch), generous line height.
- **Notation/meta:** JetBrains Mono, tiny — dates, pillar numbers, evidence labels, citations' DOIs.

### Hard rules — premium, not "AI slop"
No eyebrow labels (tiny uppercase kickers above headlines); no emoji, gradient blobs, glassmorphism, icon-card rows, stock
wellness imagery or generic SaaS heroes. Typography does the work; asymmetric layouts; purposeful black space.
Motion slow and intentional, scroll-tied, respectful of `prefers-reduced-motion`. **AI imagery only for objects/atmosphere,
never people presented as real.**
