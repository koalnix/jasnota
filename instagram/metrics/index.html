Count what listens.

Five numbers, read once a week. Likes say people saw something. Saves, shares, profile visits, link taps and follows say it stayed with them. Targets below are starting ranges for an account of ~1.4k followers that was private and personal until 31 October; recalibrate them after week two.

Tuesdays, 25 minutes

The review is a bar of 7/8 too. Tuesday at 10:00, never Sunday (that’s the rest) and never Monday night (posts need 12 hours). One notebook page per week.

Two numbers

  1. Saves per 100 reached for last week’s best and worst post.
  2. Link taps for the week, from Insights and the site analytics.

Two observations

  1. What did people say back? Read every comment, reply and DM from the week. Copy the best line.
  2. Where did the first second fail? For each reel: retention at 3 s. Below 60%, the opening frame needs a change.

Three decisions

  1. Keep: one thing to repeat next week (a format, a time, a kind of first line).
  2. Change: one thing to do differently, only one.
  3. Ask: one question for Tuesday’s story, taken from what people said.

Decision rules

  1. A carousel saved by more than 3 of every 100 reached becomes a journal entry or a Psycho-note follow-up within three weeks.
  2. A reel with under 30% non-follower reach gets a new first frame next time: start on the sound, not on the title.
  3. A reel that reaches but brings no follows: the profile, not the reel, is the problem. Check the first row and the bio’s first line.
  4. Link taps under 15 in a week: move the link sticker to the first story frame and say what is behind it in five words.
  5. Any DM asking for therapy gets the saved reply, the same day. Count them: more than five a month means the bio or a caption is promising too much.
  6. Never change the posting time or the rhythm before week four. Four weeks of one rhythm say more than four experiments.
