Не само следбеници.

The strategy’s own measures of progress: continuity, source quality, clarity of identity, relevant interactions and professional contacts. There are no follower targets here. Follower count is read for context, never as the goal.

Пет показатели

Each has one question, where to read it, and what “good” looks like for one person writing one careful piece a week.

Континуитет

Did the week’s post go up, on Monday, at 19:30?

Read: the log below. Good: 12 of 12. A late post counts; a skipped week is noted with its reason. A paused week, announced in a story, is better than a rushed one.

Квалитет на изворите

Did the post pass the nine-point source checklist before publishing?

Read: the checklist per post. Good: 9 of 9, every week. Also count corrections made (visible and dated) and errors readers found.

Јасност на идентитетот

Do people describe Jasnota the way the strategy does?

Read: how others introduce it in shares, comments and messages. Good: “психологија на музиката, со извори”. Watch for: “психолог за консултации” (the boundary is unclear) or “мотивација” (the tone drifted).

Релевантни интеракции

Is the content useful enough to keep and pass on, and does it start real questions?

Read: saves and shares per 100 accounts reached (post Insights), plus comments and DMs that ask a real question or add knowledge. Compared post to post, never against a target.

Професионални контакти

Did Jasnota connect with people who work on this?

Read: the contacts column and the mapping list. Count researchers, teachers, therapists, ensembles and institutions who wrote, replied, shared or were contacted with a reason. Good: one meaningful contact every two or three weeks.

Четврток, 20 минути

The review is a bar of 7/8 too: two numbers, two readings, three decisions. Thursday at 10:00, when Monday’s post has had three days. One line in the log per week; nothing else to maintain.

Two numbers

  1. Saves + shares per 100 reached for this week’s post, next to last week’s.
  2. Meaningful comments and questions: count only the ones that ask something real or add knowledge.

Two readings

  1. What did people ask? Read every comment and DM. Copy the best question into the ideas list (for the Friday question box or a future post).
  2. Did anything need a boundary? Therapy requests answered with /помош; if there are more than a few a month, check whether a caption or the bio promises too much.

Three decisions

  1. Keep one thing that worked (a kind of first line, a template detail, a story).
  2. Fix one thing, only one: a correction, a clearer slide, a better source.
  3. Reach out to one person or institution from the mapping whose work the next post touches.

Decision rules

  1. Never trade a source for a deadline. If the checklist isn’t complete on Sunday, the post moves a week and a story says so.
  2. A correction is published the same week it is found, as a dated comment on the post and, if substantial, in a story.
  3. A post saved noticeably more than the series average becomes a candidate for an English version or a longer web text.
  4. Don’t change the time or the templates before week 6. Six weeks of one rhythm say more than six experiments.
  5. No follower targets, no paid promotion, no follow-for-follow. Growth is a side effect of usefulness and of professional contacts.

Facebook: до текстот

Facebook’s job is to bring readers to the article, so its numbers are about the link, not about likes. Read them on Thursday next to Instagram’s, from Meta Business Suite → Insights → Content (or the post’s “See insights”).

Кликови на линкот

How many people opened the article from the post?

Read: “Link clicks” on the post. Also count clicks per 100 reached (CTR) to compare weeks fairly. Good: the CTR holds or grows across the series.

Досег

How many accounts saw it?

Read: “Reach” (people, not impressions). Context only, never a target. A week with photos and a week with the link card can be compared here.

Споделувања

Did someone pass it on?

Read: “Shares”. On Facebook a share usually carries the article’s link card to a new audience, so it is the most valuable number after clicks.

Every link carries utm_source=facebook|instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<slug>. The website has no analytics yet, so the tags are invisible until a cookie-free counter is added; until then the channel numbers come from Meta’s Insights, and the tags are ready for later.
