FollowMessage

Seven doesn’t divide into three. That is the point.

The grid is planned in bars of seven posts, grouped 2 + 2 + 3 like the 7/8 meter, laid over three columns. Each bar opens on a light downbeat (paper instead of black): posts 1, 8 and 15. Because seven never lines up with three, the accent moves one column every bar and walks diagonally across the grid — on 30 November it runs exactly corner to corner.

  • Downbeat — first post of every bar, on paper. Posts 1, 8, 15.
  • Groups of 2 — words: quotes, notation, Psycho-note covers. Black.
  • Group of 3 — images: low-key photographs and reel covers.
  1. Black is the default. A paper tile appears once every seven posts and nowhere else.
  2. One idea, one italic word. Cormorant Light for the line; italic only for the word that carries the feeling.
  3. Notation as footnote. JetBrains Mono for tempo, time, series number — bottom of the frame, never above a headline.
  4. Cinnabar once per bar, at most. A record label, a breath dot, a wrong note. If you notice it from across the room, it’s too much.
  5. Images live in the long group. Three image posts in a row give the grid its rest, like the long beat of 7/8.
  6. Mind the 3:4 crop. The profile grid shows posts at 3:4. Keep type 34 px clear of the sides on 4:5 posts.
  7. Pins are an anacrusis. Three pick-up notes before the bar: who · try · keep. See profile.

Formats and templates

03 / 05

Reusable frames. Specs are at export size (1080 px wide). Dashed lines show margins, the 3:4 grid crop and the zones the interface covers. English carries the frame; Macedonian sits beneath in grey italic when a slide needs both.

Quote, 4:5

The workhorse. A single sentence, set low and left so the top of the frame stays silent. The mark sits top-left like a clef; tempo or series as a footnote. One italic phrase per post. The Macedonian line is optional on singles (the caption carries it) and always grey. If the line needs more than four lines at this size, it belongs in a carousel.

1080 × 1350 · margin 86 px (8%) · 3:4 crop keeps 34 px each side
Cormorant Light 300 · 112–160 px · leading 0.95 · tracking −1%
JetBrains Mono 300 · 34 px · ash #8C8A85
Background ink #0A0A0B, or paper #F6F4EF for the bar’s downbeat

Image and sound

04 / 05

Shoot like you are listening: one light source, deep blacks that keep detail, grain you can feel. Hands, objects, water, rooms, the stage — Jasna herself in profile and in rim light, not performing feelings for the camera. Every photo slot the month needs is listed in the month plan.

Light

One practical or window source. No ring lights, no fill. If a face is in frame, at least half of it is in shadow. Shoot at night or at blue hour.

Colour

Blacks crushed to #0A0A0B with texture left in the shadows. Desaturate 40–60%; highlights warm toward bone #ECE8E1, shadows neutral. No teal-orange, no purple.

Motion & sound

Locked-off or slow handheld, 25 fps, cuts on 2 + 2 + 3. Sound first: her voice, room tone, the instrument close. Licensed music only when it is the subject, via the in-app library. On-screen text in Onest, lower third: English, Macedonian beneath.

Reels, shot by shot

Cinematic, slow, sound-led. Shot lengths follow 2 + 2 + 3: every reel is three acts in the proportion of the meter. Month one’s five reels are fully scripted in the plan; these two are for month two.

Decided

Handle

Rename @jasna.dzasna to @jasnota and keep her ~1.4k followers; switch to a public Creator account. Fallbacks @jasnota.mk, @jasna.nota. Steps in profile.

Her face

Yes, sparingly: the profile picture, slide 2 of the introduction, the stage, and her own voice on reels. Always low-key, never posed for a feeling.

Credentials and limits

Jasna is a psychologist and a Gestalt counsellor, a vocalist with Orchestra DIVA, and co-author of a 2024 study on perfectionism, self-esteem and impostor feelings among gifted students (findings stated only when she supplies them). Jasnota does not offer sessions: no booking links, ever. Psychology posts end with “This explains; it isn’t therapy.” The Jasna highlight says where to find help.

Music rights

Reels lead with her own voice and recordings, original sound, or traditional songs she sings herself. Otherwise the in-app licensed library only; Song of the week uses the music sticker, never re-uploaded audio. Orchestra footage only with DIVA’s permission.

Pinned posts

Three, as an anacrusis before the grid: №02 who, №03 try, and the month’s most-saved carousel to keep. Phased in from 9 November.

Languages
