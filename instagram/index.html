СледиПорака

Three tones, one per pillar. The grid reads as an index.

With one post a week, the old 7/8 diagonal no longer means anything. The grid is now coded by pillar tone, so a visitor can tell the three perspectives apart from across the room, and the calendar’s own rhythm (a long read, then a short “What does science say?” piece that answers it) makes the grid alternate dark and paper like strong and weak beats.

  • I · Ink — Психологијата на музиката. Black, bone type. Weeks 3, 6, 11.
  • II · Ink + stave — Психолошкиот свет на музичарот. Five hairline stave lines cross the cover. Weeks 1, 5, 8, 10.
  • III · Paper — „Што вели науката?“ The whole carousel on paper, like a reading card. Weeks 2, 4, 7, 9, 12.
  1. Typography is the image. Cormorant Light for the headline, one italic word; Onest for the explanation; JetBrains Mono only for notation: the footer, labels, DOIs.
  2. The footer is the system. Every slide ends on the same line: the 7/8 pillar glyph (2 + 2 + 3 dots, the pillar’s group filled), a label, the slide counter. Nothing ever sits above a headline.
  3. Evidence is visible. Every claim on a slide carries one of four labels: силни докази · умерени докази · рани/ограничени докази · отворено прашање.
  4. The last slide is always Извори, on paper: short APA, DOI in mono, the ethics line on psychology posts.
  5. Cinnabar once, on purpose. Only the verdict stamp of a myth post. Two of twelve covers stay free of it; the stamp is on slide 2.
  6. Mind the 3:4 crop. The profile grid shows posts at 3:4. Cover type stays 8% inside the edge, so the grid crop never touches it.
  7. No photo dependence. Two AI-generated atmosphere images remain (backstage door, sheet music), darkened and labelled as illustrations, on one slide and one story.

Шаблони по формат

03 / 06

Four formats, four strict sequences, one family. Everything is HTML/CSS at 4:5 (1080 × 1350), built from structured data, so a new week means new words, never new layouts. Tap any slide to see it large with its alt text.

Type on a slide

Cover headline Cormorant Light 105–260 px, leading 0.92. Inner headline 78–91 px. Body Onest 300 at 46 px (4.25% of width), ≤ 230 characters. Mono 30 px for footer, labels and DOIs.

Words on a slide

One idea per slide. Numbers with range and population (“16,5%–60%, depending on how it is measured”). Macedonian quotes „…“, decimal comma, English term in brackets on first use.

Alt text

Generated from the same data as the slide, in Macedonian: slide number, background, every word on the slide, the evidence label and the source. Pasted per slide under Advanced settings → Accessibility.

Monday, 19:30. The same minute every week.

Why Monday. The calendar is built on Mondays; a new carousel at the start of the week has seven days to be read, saved and shared before the next one, and the week’s stories hang off it.

Why 19:30. A carousel of seven text slides needs two or three quiet minutes. Early evening in Macedonia is after work, school and most classes, and before concerts and late rehearsals begin; saves, the strongest sign a post is useful, come in the first evening and the following morning. It is also a time one person can keep every week, which is what continuity means.

When to change it. Not before week 6. Then look at Insights → Total followers → Most active times and move once, by at most an hour, only if the evening peak is clearly elsewhere.

Стории, лесно

05 / 06

Twelve frame designs, 9:16, no face, no video. Each week reuses four of them; the rest live in highlights. Interface zones (top and bottom 13%) stay clear. Questions are answered publicly only anonymously and with the sender’s permission.

Reels: later, optional

Not needed in phase one. If and when there is time: a 20–30 s animated text-only explainer made from an existing carousel (the cover, three claims with their labels, the sources slide), with no voice and no face, and original or no music.

English, later

Every caption already ends with one English line. After week 12, the strongest two or three carousels get a full English version as their own posts (same templates, English type set), not mixed into the Macedonian ones.

Next series: who works on this in Macedonia

From research/mapping: institutions and published work only, e.g. the doctoral thesis on stage fright (FMU Skopje, 2018) or the regional overview of music psychology. Ask everyone named before tagging; never recommend practitioners or services.
