Пакет за објава.

Everything for one week on one card, in the order it goes out: 1) the article on the website, 2) Instagram, 3) Facebook. Copy buttons put the exact text on the clipboard, links included. Ticks stay in this browser only.

Data: research/content-bank/calendar.json → node instagram/tools/sync-social.mjs → instagram/social.js. Article pages and their link-preview images: node website/tools/import-content.mjs and node website/tools/build.mjs, then the sync again.

Веб, па мрежите.
