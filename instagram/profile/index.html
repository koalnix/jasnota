Rename the account she has. Keep the 1.4k people who already listen.

Recommendation: rebrand @jasna.dzasna into @jasnota, switch it to a public Creator account, archive the old posts. A new account starts at zero with no history; this one starts with about 1,400 people who already know her voice: friends, colleagues, students, Orchestra DIVA audiences in Bitola. That is the warmest possible first audience for a launch row, and Instagram shows new posts to followers first.

The cost is real but small. Some personal followers will not care about the new account and will drift away; expect 2–5% unfollows in the first fortnight and do not chase them. Her private life moves to a fresh private account under the old name once Instagram releases it (step 10).

If @jasnota is taken: @jasnota.mk, then @jasna.nota. Instagram handles are Latin only, so the Cyrillic јаснота lives in the posts and in #јаснота.

Pinned posts: an anacrusis

Pinned posts jump to the top row and break the diagonal. So they are treated as an anacrusis: the pick-up notes before the first downbeat. Three pins, read left to right as who · try · keep, then the 7/8 grid begins underneath.

  1. 2–8 Nov: no pins. The launch row is the top row anyway.
  2. From Mon 9 Nov: pin №02, I’m Jasna. The front door.
  3. From Mon 16 Nov: add №03, Breathing in 7/8. Something to do in 35 seconds.
  4. From Mon 30 Nov: the third pin is the month’s best carousel by saves per reach. Default №07, Why sad songs make us calmer; if №10 does better, pin that.

Re-pin in that order so the row reads who · try · keep. Never pin a single quote: it reads as a slogan out of context.

Two languages, one caption


    

    

      
Pattern: English in full, then the 7/8 divider (·· ·· ···), then the Macedonian in full, then 3–8 hashtags. Every post, no exceptions.

      
Why not Macedonian in the first comment. Comments are not part of the post: they sink below replies, they don’t travel when the post is shared to stories or sent in a DM, and they look like an afterthought to the people the brand comes from. Instagram’s own translation of Macedonian is still clumsy, so the second language has to be written, not generated.

      
Why English first. Only the first ~125 characters show before “more”, and they decide whether a stranger keeps reading. English is the brand’s default; the first line of every caption is written to stand alone.

      
Each language is written, not translated. Same thought, own rhythm. Macedonian uses ти. No emoji; the divider is the only symbol.

      
Psychology posts end with a quiet line: “This explains; it isn’t therapy.” / „Ова објаснува; не е терапија.“ Posts that touch distress add where to find help.

      
Alt text on every image, written as a description of what is there, including the words on the slide.
