Свој профил, свој глас.

Jasnota gets its own account; Jasna’s personal account stays personal. Everything a visitor sees before the first carousel: handle, name, bio, category, link, picture, highlights. Each choice has a reason; the reasons are short.

@jasnota

fallbacks: @jasnota.mk · @jasnota_mk

A platform, not a person. The account says what it is before it says who writes it.

Recommendation: create a new professional account, @jasnota. The strategy describes an informative, educational platform with its own identity, a text-first voice and no need for personal exposure. A separate account keeps that promise: the grid is only Jasnota, the followers came for the subject, and the numbers in Insights measure the platform, not a private life.

@jasna.dzasna stays personal. Jasna may share Jasnota’s posts to her own stories when she wants to, and can add “уредува @jasnota” to her personal bio if she likes. Nothing from her personal account moves to Jasnota, and nothing is archived or renamed.

The cost of starting at zero is real but fits the strategy: success here is continuity, source quality and relevant conversations, not follower counts. The first readers will come from her own share, from musicians and teachers who find a post useful, and from professionals reached through the mapping.

Instagram handles are Latin only, so the Cyrillic Јаснота lives in the name field (searchable) and in #јаснота.

Creating the account, step by step

  1. Thu 29 OctCheck the handle. In the app: profile menu → Add account → Create new account; type jasnota. If it is taken, use @jasnota.mk, then @jasnota_mk. Use a dedicated address (zdravo@jasnota.mk or a new mailbox), not her personal one; turn on two-factor authentication.
  2. Thu 29 OctKeep it separate. Adding the account to her Accounts Center makes switching easy; turn off cross-posting and “share to Facebook” so nothing from either account appears on the other by accident.
  3. Fri 30 OctProfessional account: Business, category Education (details below). Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. Show the category on the profile.
  4. Fri 30 OctDress the profile: name, bio, link, picture (the Ψ mark, below). Contact button: email only. No booking or “reserve” action buttons, ever.
  5. Fri 30 OctSafety and messages. Hidden words on (comments and message requests); comment filter for abuse. Add the four saved replies (below). Message requests stay open: questions from musicians and professionals are the point.
  6. Sat 31 OctHighlights before launch. Post the six method and about frames as stories, then save them into Како работиме and За Јаснота and set the covers. The pillar, concept and myth highlights fill as the weeks go.
  7. Sun 1 NovSchedule week 1. Carousel for Monday 19:30 with its alt text; the teaser story for 12:00. Read the sources slide one last time against the DOIs.
  8. Mon 2 NovLaunch. After it is live, Jasna may share it from @jasna.dzasna with one sentence of her own. Reply to every comment that evening.
  9. from week 2Introduce it to professionals, quietly. From the mapping: one message a week to a researcher, teacher or institution whose published work a post cites or touches. No mass follow, no follow-for-follow.

Account type and category

Education. Never “Psychologist”: under a brand name it reads as a practice and invites session requests.

Account type: Business. Jasnota is a publication, not a public figure. Business gives the same Insights, scheduling and saved replies; it limits the licensed music library, which this plan does not use. (Creator is also fine if she prefers it; the category matters more.)

Categories Instagram offers that fit (the list is searched in-app and changes over time; check the exact wording when switching):

  • EducationPlain, accurate, shows as “Образование” to Macedonian users. Recommended.
  • Education websiteGood alternative once jasnota.mk is the main home.
  • Science, Technology & EngineeringSignals rigour, but reads as tech; second choice.
  • Media / news company · MagazinePossible for a publication, but promises news.
  • Psychologist · Mental health serviceAvoid: implies a practice, sessions and clinical responsibility.
  • Musician / band · ArtistAvoid: the personal-artist frame the strategy retired.

Седум highlights

Covers are typography and brand signs on a circle, in the pillar tones: no photos, no icon packs. Order reads like a visit: the three perspectives, the two series, how we work, who we are. Labels are Macedonian; “Како работиме” may truncate on small phones (fallback: “Метод”).

Опис, македонски прв


    

      
Pattern: Macedonian in full, then the 7/8 divider (·· ·· ···), one English line, then 3–6 hashtags. Every post.

      
The first line is the question, written to stand alone before “more”. Calm and precise: it promises exactly what the carousel delivers.

      
Evidence and ethics travel with the post. The caption repeats the evidence label for the main claim, and psychology posts carry “Ова не е дијагноза ни терапија” with a referral to a psychologist, psychotherapist or doctor.

      
Hashtags: #јаснота always; one or two Macedonian topic tags; two professional English tags (#musicpsychology, #musicperformanceanxiety, #musictherapy…) that researchers and teachers actually follow. Never more than six.

      
Language: “ти” in captions and stories; quotes „…“; decimal comma; English term in brackets on first use.

Settings → Business tools and controls → Saved replies. Type the shortcut in a message and the full text appears. Therapy requests get a kind boundary and a referral, the same day; never advice. If a message suggests someone is in danger, the reply includes 112.
