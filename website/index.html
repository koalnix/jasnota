Manifesto

Every feeling has its own tone.

Some are loud.

Others are heard only in the pause

between two breaths.

Clarity is not an answer.

It is a way of listening.

Three voices,
one note.

Not three topics. Three movements of the same piece.

My name means clear.

I'm a psychologist and a Gestalt counsellor, and I have been singing for as long as I can remember. For a long time I thought they were two separate worlds: one for explaining, the other for feeling.

Jasnota is where I stopped keeping them apart. Here I write and talk about what happens inside us, with a song instead of a diagnosis and a pause instead of a quick answer.

I'm a psychologist, but Jasnota is not therapy, and not a substitute for it. It can be a good way to begin the conversation.
