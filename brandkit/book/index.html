Jasna is the founder’s name. In Macedonian, jasna also means clear, bright, lucid. A nota is a musical note. Join the two words and what remains inside is jas, “I”, beside nota: the self and the sound in one word, which is what the brand does.

Јасна е името на авторката. На македонски, јасна значи и чиста, светла, разбирлива. Нота е звук запишан на хартија. Кога двата збора ќе се спојат, во средината останува јас до нота: личноста и звукот во еден збор, токму она што го прави брендот.

To hear yourself.

A calm, intelligent voice that listens: feelings explained through sound, and sound through feelings.

A personal brand by Jasna, at the crossing of psychology, music and human experience. Think of it as a liner note for the inner life.

Jasnota is

  • explanation, grounded and plain
  • a song instead of a diagnosis, a pause instead of a quick answer

  • a music channel
  • motivational content
  • “calm wellness”: no beaches, no sparkles

1.4

Attunement.

камертон

In psychology, attunement is when one person tunes in to another’s inner state.

A tuning fork is literally an instrument of attunement, and its silhouette is almost the Greek Ψ, the symbol of psychology. That is where the mark comes from.

Во психологијата, усогласувањето е кога еден човек ќе се наштима на внатрешната состојба на друг. Камертонот е инструмент за усогласување, а силуетата му е речиси Ψ.

One fork is struck; the other, untouched, starts to answer. 440 Hz.

1.5

Seven eighths.

седум осмини

Macedonian folk music lives in uneven meters: 7/8, 9/8, 11/16. Human experience is uneven too.

7/8 is counted 2 + 2 + 3: short, short, long. The grouping returns as a structural motif in grids, spacing, animation timing and the 02 sign. Uneven, yet always in time.

Нерамномерното не е грешка, туку ритам. Кратко, кратко, долго.

V.

1.9

Speak with, not at.

Calm, precise, warm, a bit literary. Short sentences. One idea per post.

  • 01“You” in English; ти, the singular, in Macedonian.
  • 02Specific, slightly poetic. Musical words when they explain: tempo, rest, key, a wrong note.
  • 03No exclamation marks, no emoji, no “unlock”, “journey”, “transform”, “empower”.
  • 04Explain, never diagnose. End with the line: “This is not therapy.” / „Ова не е терапија.“

Say

  • Sadness has its own tempo. You don’t have to speed it up.

    Тагата има свое темпо. Не мора да ја забрзаш.

  • Some days are in 7/8. That isn’t a mistake.

    Некои денови се во 7/8. Тоа не е грешка.

  • A wrong note is not a wrong person.

    Погрешна нота не е погрешен човек.

Don’t say

  • Unlock your full potential!

    Empty promise, exclamation mark.

  • Your journey to a better life starts today.

    “Journey” is a cliché.

  • 5 hacks for instant happiness

    Happiness isn’t a hack, and speed isn’t ours.

  • Book your session now.

    Jasnota offers no sessions.

2.1

A tuning fork that reads as Ψ.

01 Камертон / Ψ · primary mark · chosen by Jasna, 28 Oct 2026

  1. 01Psychology and music in one form. Ψ and tuning fork read at the same time, without explanation.
  2. 02It contains the pause. The inner stroke stops before the bowl: listening without touching, the quiet moment before an answer.
  3. 03It is vertical, like the ј. It stands beside the word without competing with it.
  4. 04It survives 16 pixels, an avatar, and blind embossing in paper. No other direction is as ownable.
  5. 05The other five directions are kept as the brand’s vocabulary: the secondary signs on page .

5.2

A small vocabulary.

Staff lines

Five graphite hairlines, a place for one note. A background for a quote or a cover, never a repeating pattern.

Waveform

Vertical bars with round caps: the played part in bone, the rest in ash at 38%. For players, reels, episode covers.

shown at 4× the normal strength

Grain

Fine film noise over black, 5–8% opacity on screen. It may step (six frames in 1.6 s) or stay still. Never on logo files.

One per frame: the preloader of the site, the dot of the ј, a note on the staff. In print, a plain dot without the halo.

6.1

Slow, like a film cut.

  • 010.8–1.6 s for anything that enters. Never bouncy, never springy.
  • 02Tied to scroll like a film cut: scrubbed, not triggered. Not everything fades up.
  • 03Reduced motion: every animation becomes a still state. Sound never autoplays.

A · The mark draws itself — bowl, then stem, then the inner stroke, which stops before the bowl.

B · Page transition — hairlines draw, five ink bands close, the mark, the bands open from the middle.

C · Scroll-scrubbed reveal — words rise from 10% to full as the scroll passes, 1.8 words of overlap.
